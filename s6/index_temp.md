## 🏆 FRIENDLY NEIGHBOURHOOD MODE + GUANTLET CHALLENGE

> **Friendly Neighbourhood Mode for everybody:** For this season, Friendly Neighbourhood Mode is the default scoring for all kouples. Every game count toward your score, where you can try as many times as you like to win at each difficulty. Your score in each scenario is your highest difficulty win.

> **Gauntlet Challenge:** In addition to the above, kouples wanting a little extra pressure can participate in the Gauntlet Challenge. Once per difficulty level, per scenario, you can specify ahead of time that you are going to take on the Gauntlet Challenge with that game. Your score in each scenario is your highest difficulty Gauntlet Challenge win.


## ⚔️ ROUND 601: INFINITE PRELATES

### Thanos + Menagerie Medley + Band of Badoon + Prelates + Modified Masters of Evil 🂡 (Base difficulty = 2☆)

**CONDITION 1:** Prelates deck
> **Setup:** Shuffle the Prelate modular set and place it Prelate face up. This is the Prelates deck.

> Throughout the game the top card will be your next Prelate.

> Defeated Prelates go face up on the bottom of the Prelate deck instead of to the Victory Display.

> Each Prelate LOSES its text related to engaging with the first player.

> Each Prelate gains the following text: "**Forced Response:** When this Prelate minion enters play, draw the top card of the Masters of Evil deck and assign it this Prelate minion before any other enters play effects trigger. The Masters of Evil minion loses its card type and is no longer a card that can be targeted by game effects. All non-trait text from the Master of Evil minion's text box is considered to be additional text in the Prelate minion's text box." Abilities that trigger when the Masters of Evil minion would have activated, such as Melter's ability that triggers when he attacks, trigger for the Prelate during their same type of activation.

**CONDITION 2:** Thanos' **Forced Response** is replaced with "**Forced Response:** After the infinity stone deck runs out, reveal the top card of the Prelate deck and give it to the active player." This will most commonly occur during one of Thanos' activations, which could result in the Prelate minion activating during the Enemies Activate portion of the Villain Phase. Fun!

## ⚔️ ROUND 602: PRELATE BREAKOUT

### M.O.D.O.K. + Dangerous Recruits + Annoying Attachments 🂡 (Base difficulty = 2☆)

**CONDITION 1:** Holding Cells
> **Setup:** Remove the wild resource HOLDING CELL (M.O.D.O.K. #5) from the game. There will be only 3 holding cells in the game

> The Forced Interrupt on each HOLDING CELL is replaced with "**Forced Interrupt:** When the last lock counter is removed from here, remove this card from play and put the top Prelate from the Prelate deck in play engaged with the first player. Attach assigned attachments to his Prelate."

> Add the following text to M.O.D.O.K.  "**Forced Interrupt:**" At the end of Stage 1 of the Villain Phase remove a lock counter from the HOLDING CELL with the fewest lock counters (players choose if tied) and flip over an Annoying Attachment and assign it to the top Prelate in the Prelate deck. Players do not need to execute this Forced Interrupt if they have removed one or more lock counters by an effect other than this forced interrupt since the previous Villain Phase Stage 1." For example, if you defeated an ADAPTOID during the previous Villain Phase or the most recent Player Phase, you do not need to execute this Forced Interrupt. It is recommended to use a Yes/No style of counter that you flip to Yes when the players (not this Forced Interrupt) remove a counter from a HOLDING CELL, and then this counter flips to No at the end of Villain Phase Stage 1.


**CONDITION 2:** Prelates deck
> **Setup:** Shuffle the Prelate modular set and place it Prelate face up. This is the Prelates deck.

> Throughout the game the top card will be your next Prelate. Whenever a new Prelate becomes the top card of this deck (including the start of the game), flip over X+1 Annoying Attachments and assign them to this Prelate, where X = the number of Holding Cells that have been removed from the game. When this Prelate enters play, all its assigned attachments will be attached to it.

> Each Prelate RETAINS its text related to engaging with the first player.

## 📝 THE DRAFT

### ⚙️ Draft Details

| 🎮 **Feature** | 📋 **Details** |
|----------------|----------------|
| 👥 **Division Size** | Maximum 12 kouples per division |
| 📚 **Draft Groups** | 2 hero-pair groups + 2 aspect-pair groups |
| 📦 **Pool Size** | 3 more than needed per draft group |
| 🦸‍♂️ **Hero Pool** | Hero pairs, partnering stronger heroes with weaker hereos. <br>All heroes through Wonder Man & Hercules (no bans) |
| 🌈 **Aspect Pool** | Aspect pairs, with two-of-a-kind less likely |
| 🚫 **Pick Restrictions** | 1 pick per draft group |
| ⏱️ **Pick Timer** | 24 hours per selection |
| 🤖 **Auto-Pick** | Draft-bot chooses if you miss your window |
| 🐍 **Draft Format** | 4 rounds, snake order (A→B→C then C→B→A) |

**Further draft notes:**
> * **Spider-Woman:** If Spider-Woman is added to the pool, she will have a pre-selected aspect that you must use, and then her other aspect will be whichever of your drafted aspects that you assign to her.<br>
> * **Adam Warlock:** If you draft Adam Warlock, the aspect that you assign to him must be one of the four aspects that you use to build his deck.
